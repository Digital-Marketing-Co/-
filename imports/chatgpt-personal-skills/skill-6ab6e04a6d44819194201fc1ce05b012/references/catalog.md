# Prompt catalog

Default marketing ordering reflects potential reach and reuse, not a measured universal ranking. Reorder for the user's goal, channel, budget, and evidence.

| Rank | Output type | Invocation example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Custom web app or interactive tool | `/generate-prompt web-app stack=Next.js,React,Prisma,TypeScript,Tailwind` |
| 2 | Website or conversion landing page | `/generate-prompt landing-page domain=gnitekram.org` |
| 3 | Video ad, brand film, or demo | `/generate-prompt video duration=30s aspect=9:16 style=awe-inspiring` |
| 4 | Campaign strategy and channel plan | `/generate-prompt campaign audience=founders` |
| 5 | Interactive presentation | `/generate-prompt presentation audience=investors` |
| 6 | Campaign image or illustration | `/generate-prompt image aspect=16:9 style=futuristic` |
| 7 | Search content hub or editorial series | `/generate-prompt content-hub topic=...` |
| 8 | Email or lifecycle sequence | `/generate-prompt email stages=5` |
| 9 | Social creative variants | `/generate-prompt social platform=...` |
| 10 | Brand identity and design system | `/generate-prompt brand values=...` |
| 11 | Data story or infographic | `/generate-prompt data-story source=...` |
| 12 | Audio narrative or podcast | `/generate-prompt audio duration=...` |
| 13 | Proposal or pitch | `/generate-prompt proposal buyer=...` |
| 14 | Experiment or analytics dashboard | `/generate-prompt experiment metric=...` |

For academic presentation, rank: (1) evidence synthesis or review protocol; (2) research paper or grant; (3) reproducible analysis or research app; (4) conference talk or thesis defense; (5) scientific figure or poster; (6) teaching module or annotated video; (7) dataset or methodological appendix. Support books, courses, documentaries, animation, games, spreadsheets, surveys, technical specs, and any requested output. Verify `gnitekram.org` before inferring its mission or brand.
