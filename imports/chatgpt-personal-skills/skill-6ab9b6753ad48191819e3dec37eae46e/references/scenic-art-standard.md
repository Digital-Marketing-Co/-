# Scenic image-generation standard

## Default subject and style

For scenic cover, banner and illustrative imagery, favor recognizable real places on Earth: beautiful cities, national parks, coastlines, islands, forests, mountains and waterfronts. Choose the place for the adjacent subject, not as arbitrary decoration. For this owner, useful candidates include Annapolis/Chesapeake waterfronts, Swiss cities/Alps, Northern California redwoods, the Gold Coast, Caribbean islands and tropical rainforests. These are preferences, not a mandatory repeated list or a claim of objective global ranking.

Prefer real photography as a grounded reference when available and authorized. With image generation, create photorealistic AI-enhanced depictions of real places: sophisticated light, depth, clarity, optimistic prosperous atmosphere, restrained future-facing architecture or infrastructure where relevant. Preserve recognizable geography. Do not turn every place into an invented neon metropolis. A natural-history or historical subject may require an unaltered landscape or historically accurate scene instead. Explicit subject/style instructions prevail.

Distinguish an actual licensed photograph, an AI edit of that photograph, and a generated scene inspired by a real location. Record source/license when applicable, actual prompt and model/tool provenance, place, placement and modifications. Do not label invented buildings or infrastructure as current reality, and never use generated geography as exact map evidence. If exact landmark fidelity matters, obtain and inspect an appropriate reference; otherwise label the scene an AI-enhanced interpretation and disclose uncertainty.

## Context before generation

Create an image plan with unique slot ID, adjacent heading/paragraph, intended explanatory function, selected place, why it fits, image role and composition. Use scenery where it reinforces meaning; use deterministic plots, maps, diagrams or UI illustrations when those are needed for exact teaching. Do not substitute scenery for an equation, chart, evidence screenshot or requested subject. A repeated conceptual theme is allowed; repeating the same visual is not.

## No baked-in text or text artifacts

Default all scenic-generation prompts to: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks, no pseudo-writing, no signboards or invented UI labels. Place headings, captions, commands and labels as separately typeset live text or deterministic vector text with embedded/shaped fonts. Keep quiet negative space and a high-contrast backing surface for any planned overlay.

Inspect the actual generated raster at full useful resolution and at its final placed size. Reject jumbled or misspelled lettering, pseudo-text, blurred/aliased glyphs, warped signs and obfuscated text-like patterns; do not conceal them with blur, smears or arbitrary glyph replacement. Regenerate or use an authorized image edit to remove the artifact cleanly. OCR, when available, supplements visual review; do not claim OCR or text-free certification without inspecting actual output. If readable textual evidence is explicitly requested, preserve it accurately rather than altering its meaning; treat that as an explicit exception, with deterministic text preferred for designed labels.

## Uniqueness and placement

Every published image has its own source, prompt, subject/view and composition. Do not reuse a cover as a banner or a banner as an inline figure. Crops, recoloring, mirroring, filters and resizing do not make a new image. Check paths, exact hashes, perceptual similarity and a contact sheet across the whole output; inspect visual near-duplicates manually. A shared location can appear again only with a substantially different relevant scene and composition, not a disguised repeat. Repeated intentional brand insignia are distinct from editorial imagery and must be disclosed as exceptions.

Run the images skill's audit_unique_images.py over the complete published inventory. Resolve every collision; if an automated threshold flags distinct images, inspect and document a justified finding without falsely claiming the unmodified automated audit passed. Check each final page/view for correct image-to-text association, crop/aspect ratio, bleed, safe insets, contrast and artifacts. A prompt cannot guarantee a flawless image; missing visual inspection remains unverified.
