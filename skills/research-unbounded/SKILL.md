# Unlimited-Depth Research and Document Standard v1.0.0
Applies to every compatible research, atlas, textbook, WCA, Wicca, bibliography, PDF, and publication skill.

## Scope
Never impose arbitrary page, word, chapter, country, equation, figure, citation, or bibliography caps. Expand until relevant verifiable evidence is comprehensively addressed, without padding or duplicate claims. User scope and safety take precedence.
Physical model context, API, filesystem, rendering, upload, link, licensing and execution limits still apply. Overcome practical size limits with checkpointed chapters, volumes, manifests, resumable pipelines and indexed links; never promise literally unlimited capacity.

## Source standards
Search primary scholarly and official sources, systematic reviews, authoritative datasets, and research PDFs. Prioritize relevance, methods, freshness and evidentiary quality rather than institutional prestige; include Ivy League scholarship when relevant, plus global institutions. Extract exact bibliographic metadata, DOI/URL, page references, publication year, limitations and licensing. Never fabricate references, data, source access or PDF verification. Respect copyright and quote limits.

## Publication
Provide full source-to-claim traceability, bibliography, definitions, mathematical derivations, immediately following ITQE variable tables where requested, uncertainty, reproducible calculations, accessibility, and print QA. Distinguish generated imagery from documentary photography. Validate all pages for clipping, overflow, font coverage and broken links. Split into numbered volumes only when necessary and provide a master index.

## Aliases
/wicca and /Wicca are aliases for /wca. Wicca here names the research citation and bibliography workflow, not a religious subject. Combine with /wca-ivy-biblio when requested. An alias must never suppress existing WCA functionality.

## Completion
Report exact completed outputs, source coverage, unresolved gaps, tests and any repository sync status. Never imply a complete research corpus or deployed skill unless verified.
