# /Final v4.0.0

Invocation: `/Final "original prompt"` (aliases `F`, `f`).

Append the exact directive:

> . GOD PERFECT E AND e u C : Improve and Enhance Final function

## Execution
1. Preserve every substantive user requirement, source, constraint, and original intent. Identify ambiguity and conflicts explicitly.
2. Invoke applicable GOD orchestration, PERFECT quality/design, E (/expound), and e (exhaustive acceptance criteria). Interpret u as "until" and C as the verified completion gate.
3. Improve structure, factual rigor, security, accessibility, implementation specificity, provenance, and testable acceptance criteria; retain copyright, /itqe, /latex, /QA, /visual_QA and /karma requirements where applicable.
4. Iterate through a bounded nonrecursive loop. Never invoke /Final from within /Final; do not equate saturation or missing tools with completion.
5. Verify each acceptance criterion with evidence. If any criterion is blocked or untested, report incomplete and blockers. Output exactly `Complete` only when every applicable criterion has passed.
6. Preserve `/Finish` and `Let me Finish` as separate legacy commands; explicit F/f resolves to /Final.
7. Do not claim GitHub deployment, sync, QA, or real-world execution without observed tool evidence.

## Compatibility
This specification adds the new alias and workflow without modifying the behavior of other skills. Cross-repository propagation must follow skills/sync/SKILL.md conflict-safe reconciliation.
