## Questions

1. Which signals do domestic dogs produce toward other dogs, humans, and other animals, and in what contexts?
2. What can be observed directly, and what is an interpretation?
3. Which popular readings are unsupported or context-bound?
4. How do development, aging, health, morphology, and experience change the signal?
5. Which learning processes does the evidence support for humane teaching?
6. Which competencies follow from those processes, and where is the evidence only consensus?

## Inclusion

Peer-reviewed empirical papers, reviews, scholarly books, veterinary behavior texts, university repository theses when methods are reported, government publications, and named professional guidelines. Domestic dog (Canis familiaris) primary; comparative canid work only when it clarifies a dog finding.

## Exclusion

Unsourced blogs, unsourced training marketing, undated social posts, and papers whose methods cannot be recovered from the opened text. Do not exclude a study solely for HTML delivery.

## Record

Every query, date, hit count, and keep count goes in `search-log.md`. Every kept source goes in `sources.jsonl`.
