# Quality profile — etymology

## Scope
Produce sourced historical linguistic analysis of the exact user-supplied lemma or phrase.

## Acceptance criteria
- Preserve language tags, accents, IPA, hyphens, and reconstructed asterisks.
- Distinguish verified attestations from reconstructions and earliest found from earliest possible.
- Cite historical dictionaries, corpora, and scholarship; label contested hypotheses and source access limits.
- Avoid fabricated sources, citations, chronology, PDFs, or external verification.
- Check Unicode typography and bibliography consistency.

## Evidence policy
Cases remain NOT TESTED until executed with recorded artifacts. Structural validation alone does not establish linguistic accuracy or document quality.
