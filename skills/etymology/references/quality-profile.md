# Quality profile — etymology v3.0.0

## Scope
Produce sourced historical linguistic analysis of the exact user-supplied lemma or phrase, with definitions first and separate histories for every constituent word.

## Acceptance criteria
- Preserve language tags, accents, IPA, hyphens, reconstructed asterisks, and quoted targets.
- Distinguish attestation from reconstruction, cognacy from ancestry, and earliest found from actual origin.
- Define all supported senses and independently trace every meaningful constituent before dating the complete expression.
- Every historical graph edge and semantic change is cited or explicitly marked hypothetical/unknown.
- Validate chronological ordering, node references, graph acyclicity for ancestry edges, and consistent textual and visual timelines.
- Cite historical dictionaries, corpora, and scholarship; label contested hypotheses and source access limits.
- Avoid fabricated sources, citations, chronology, IPA, PDFs, QA results, or deployment claims.
- Check Unicode typography, bibliography consistency, accessible reading order, contrast, and diagram text alternatives.
- Apply /Perfect and /Final as iterative gates, but never declare C when required checks are blocked or unperformed.

## Evidence policy
Cases remain NOT TESTED until executed with recorded artifacts. Structural validation alone does not establish linguistic accuracy or document quality.
