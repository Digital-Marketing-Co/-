# Acceptance scenarios

Use these scenarios to assess instructions or a real expansion. Record actual execution evidence separately; a written scenario is not a passed document test.

1. **Earlier insertion.** Start with numeric source first appearances A, B, C. Insert source D before B. Require A=1, D=2, B=3, C=4 throughout old and new prose, tables, captions, notes where applicable, links, and bibliography. Later A citations retain 1.
2. **Move, removal, and duplicate source.** Move C ahead of A, remove the last citation to B, and add an alternate URL for A with the same verified DOI. Require a contiguous unique-work sequence C, A; no stale B entry or dangling reference; one A record, without merging distinct editions.
3. **Chicago notes.** Insert a new source in a caption between two prose citations and repeat the first work later. Require contiguous note-event numbering across chapters; a valid short note on repetition; and a separately ordered unique-work bibliography by first appearance.
4. **Nested visual expansion.** Add a section and two subsections to a document with 16:9 full-bleed banners. Require three separate new banners, preserved compliant originals, matched styles and placement, updated figure references, and no reuse of the parent banner for either child.
5. **Inline cadence and uniqueness.** Insert 1,200 words into a passage near existing illustrations. Recompute cadence across insertion boundaries and add enough distinct relevant inline illustrations that no body-prose interval exceeds 500 words or a stricter inherited limit. Banners and equation plates cannot satisfy the interval. Reject a cropped banner reused inline.
6. **Equation and pagination changes.** Insert an equation, its variable table, and a sourced diagram. Require stable equation/table coverage, global citation reconciliation, readable compiled mathematics, an attached table and caption, current cross-references, and another final audit after repagination.
7. **Design drift and blocked tools.** Supply only a PDF or make a requested validator unavailable. Require inspection and faithful reconstruction of the source design with disclosed limits; an equivalent documented audit where possible; otherwise a qualified partial result. Never claim that a missing tool ran or unseen pages were inspected.
8. **Explicit alternative style.** Supply an author-date document with an explicitly mandated alphabetical bibliography. Preserve that style, verify every reference, and do not manufacture numeric citation labels or reorder against the explicit requirement.
