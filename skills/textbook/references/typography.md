# Typography classes

- .title — EB Garamond or display face, 28–36 pt, high contrast
- .subtitle — display face, 16–20 pt
- .h1 — numbered, 18–22 pt, bold
- .h2 — numbered, 14–16 pt
- .h3 — 12–14 pt
- .paragraph — Literata or Georgia 10–11 pt, 1.4 leading
- .link — distinct color, annotation
- .citation — superscript, smaller
- .caption — italic, 9 pt
- .footer — 8 pt centered living copyright
- .bib — hanging indent, linked

Contrast: sample local background luminance; choose ink so ratio ≥ 4.5:1. Overlay if needed.
