# Design and QA contract
Create a futuristic scientific editorial system: deep navy/black backgrounds, restrained cyan/violet metallic accents, spatial depth in art, generous hierarchy, and calm text surfaces. Judge beauty through visual inspection and consistency; do not substitute a numerical score for editorial judgment. Keep body text and equations free of glow or image noise.

Every text object must carry an explicit semantic role and named style. Cover title, subtitle, author, edition/date, part title, chapter title, section title, subsection title, heading number, body, lead, definition, theorem, proof, example, exercise, solution, quote, list item, table header/cell, figure label/caption, equation/number, ITQE header/cell, citation marker, note, bibliography author/title/metadata/link, hyperlink, running head, footer legal/link, page number, TOC entry, index entry, glossary term/definition, and accessibility description. Distinct roles may share font properties, but must remain separately addressable. Implement tokens with named TeX commands/environments, document styles, or CSS classes depending on the production engine. Do not pretend a JSON registry itself renders a PDF.

Default live-text inset: 0.60 inches horizontally, 0.55 inches vertically, with extra footer clearance. Body 11–12 pt with 1.35–1.5 line spacing; captions/notes normally at least 9 pt; footer at least 8.5 pt. Use a licensed serif or legible sans body family, a display family for headings, a genuine math font, and mono for code. Embed all used fonts and audit Unicode coverage. Do not arbitrarily discard a necessary font because of file size.

Contrast procedure: compute sRGB relative luminance after actual alpha compositing. Sample the background across each text rectangle (including gradients and images), use the worst contrast result, and test production output after color conversion. Target 7:1 for body, captions, notes, citations, table text, and footer text; require at least 4.5:1 for all ordinary text and 3:1 only for genuinely large text (18 pt regular or 14 pt bold). Pick white/near-white or dark text by the minimum ratio across samples. If no candidate meets the target, adjust a backing panel/scrim or relocate text, remeasure, and record the fix. An opaque panel gives a reliable baseline. Do not describe WCAG-derived contrast checks as certification of the PDF's total accessibility. Tag structure, reading order, link purpose, and alt text require separate checks.

## Gates
- Content preflight: claim coverage, scientific reasoning, source identity, math syntax, numbering, equations/units, complete text roles, artwork plan, and exercise checks.
- Stylesheet proof: render all roles and hardest layout cases before adoption; verify font embedding, readable sizes, hierarchy, every background class, contrast, long URLs, tables, ITQE blocks, and footer clearances. Freeze a version/hash after repairs.
- Final structural scan: inspect PDF integrity/page boxes, text extraction, embedded fonts, bookmarks, TOC/page references, duplicate IDs, citation resolution, URL annotations, missing images, source-to-PDF text coverage, raw TeX, missing-glyph warnings, overset/overfull boxes, clipping, and content outside bounds. Treat automated findings as candidates needing review.
- Final visual scan: render every page at 150–200 dpi; inspect readable individual pages plus contact sheets. Check text and image collision, equation baselines and symbols, contrast beneath actual text, banner bleed, bottom bleed of full-page backgrounds, caption association, footers, table continuation headers, whitespace, orphan headings, widows, stretched artwork, and unexpected blank pages. Contact sheets alone are insufficient.
- Image audit: ensure every numbered section/subsection has a distinct banner; ensure inline art intervals are met and imagery is distinct from banners and backgrounds. Use exact hashes and perceptual similarity plus visual review, with explicit exceptions for intentionally repeated insignia only.
- Math audit: compare compiled equations to source, including fractions, tensors, matrices, nested indices, Greek, operators, delimiters, and line breaks; inspect every equation and ITQE association. No raw commands, tofu, clipped symbols, or unsupported substitutions.
- Release: record each check's method, artifact/page IDs, expected/observed result, status, fixes, and remaining limitations. Missing evidence is blocked or untested, never pass. Rerun checks after repairs and final compression. Keep the editable source and checkpoint manifest.

Manifest fields: scope, chapter_status, source_ids, style_revision, style_proof_pdf, image_inventory, equation_ids, dependency_versions, dependency_execution_results, build_command, build_log, pdf_path, qa_checks, unresolved_findings, delivery_status. Only certify checks actually performed.
