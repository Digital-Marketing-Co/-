## Workflow
1. **Scope and research.** Record topic boundaries, prerequisites, learning objectives, chapter map, terminology, search date, search strings, and accessible sources. Research primary literature and academic repositories. Prefer claim-relevant PDFs on Ivy League university sites and other academic research sites. Institutional prestige must not override relevance or evidence quality. Read sources before using them. Follow `references/research.md`.
2. **Write a textbook.** Progress from definitions and foundations through derivations, proofs where applicable, worked examples, comparisons, limitations, methods, applications, and open research questions. Give each chapter objectives, prerequisite pointers, numbered definitions, examples, graded exercises, solutions or a solution appendix, and a substantive recap. Include TOC, notation list, glossary, index where supported, bibliography, and research limits. Distinguish established facts, deductions, hypotheses, and unsupported proposals. PhD-level means evidence and reasoning, not inflated claims of exhaustive coverage.
3. **Content preflight before styling.** Audit scientific accuracy, source support, citation identity, exercise solvability, equations and units, numbering, internal references, image placement plan, and semantic-role coverage. Resolve critical findings before using the production stylesheet. Record results in QA.
4. **Calibrate the stylesheet.** Read `references/design-qa.md`. Use `assets/styles.json` as the semantic token registry. Create a representative proof PDF containing a dense page, long bibliography entry, long caption, table, multi-line equation, ITQE table, footer, banner, and full-page background. Measure contrast and inspect layout before freezing the style revision. Test each distinct background type and font family. Do not apply an untested stylesheet throughout the book.
5. **Produce artwork and mathematics.** Generate a unique 16:9 banner for every section/subsection and separate contextual inline illustrations. Maintain image IDs, prompts, placement, dimensions, hashes, and similarity findings. Preserve aspect ratio, captions, and attribution. Use generated artwork for concepts; use deterministic vector tools for exact charts and scientific diagrams. Label conceptual illustrations. Compile real TeX with a math-capable engine and compatible math font. Show true fractions, radicals, delimiters, matrices, tensor indices, Greek symbols, limits, and aligned equations. Put an ITQE Identifier–Term–Quantity–Explanation table immediately after every displayed equation; include units, domain, meaning, and assumptions; do not fabricate values. Static PDF tables supplement any interactive companion.
6. **Lay out and compile.** Place backgrounds independently behind content. Make backgrounds and top banners touch left/right/top page edges. Full-page background images also touch bottom. Preserve a safe inset for readable live text; full bleed applies to artwork, not clipping text at the edge. Size full-page artwork for the page ratio without stretching; use a deliberate crop or newly generated matching artwork. Preserve 16:9 banner ratio. If physical printing is requested, add the printer's bleed outside TrimBox; digital edge-to-edge artwork alone is not a print bleed specification. Embed permitted fonts, keep text selectable, and create PDF bookmarks, working citations, TOC links, and cross-references.
7. **Final QA and repair.** Follow all gates in `references/design-qa.md`, including every page at readable resolution. Run dependency render checks and `scripts/contrast.py` on actual background samples. Repair defects, recompile, and repeat affected checks; recheck pagination and references globally after layout changes. Run the applicable negative-language final scan without changing exact evidence or mathematical meaning. Record pass/fail/blocked with evidence, not unsupported all-pass claims.
8. **Deliver.** Require zero unresolved critical defects. Report material source or tool limits and unfinished checks. Save the actual PDF and requested companions. A blocked draft must be labeled draft. Continue autonomously until complete or a real dependency/input blocker prevents progress.
