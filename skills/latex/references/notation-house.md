# Notation house rules

Explain every variable, subscript, and constant the first time an expression appears. Example

The Planck constant is h = 6.626 070 15 x 10^-34 J s. Here h is the quantum of action, J is the joule, and s is the second.

Do not invent a value. Quote BIPM, NIST, or CODATA when the number is a constant.

Display math is for identities the reader must inspect. Inline math is for a single quantity inside a sentence. Do not promote every unit to a display plate.

Every display equation, in chat or in a file, is followed immediately by an ITQE table — Identifier, Term, Quantity or unit, Explanation. The `/ivy-biblio` skill owns the table contract and the Folio builder draws it. This skill still compiles the plate. Do not ship a bare equation.

Chemical formulae may stay Unicode (`CO2`, `H2O`) when the face has subscript digits. Otherwise use a plate.
