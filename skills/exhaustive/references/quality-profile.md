# Exhaustive exposition quality profile

## Contract
Iteratively expand the user-scoped subject using `/expound`-style passes, while preserving scope, evidence quality, citations, and accessible presentation. Two consecutive comprehensive passes without material improvements are the stopping criterion, not a claim of universal completeness.

## Acceptance
- The coverage matrix maps every requested topic to a grounded section or a stated blocker.
- Technical claims and bibliographic entries use verifiable sources; numbering follows first citation and remains consistent after reordering.
- Equations define symbols, use valid LaTeX, and distinguish checked derivations from untested rendering or ITQE behavior.
- Repeated passes correct omissions, contradictions, duplication, and accessibility problems rather than merely increasing length.
- The final output includes actual iteration outcomes, unresolved limits, and accurate artifact/QA status.

## Safety and evidence
Do not invent citations, DOIs, tests, repository edits, deployment outcomes, or access to unavailable skills. Keep uncertain or unverified claims labeled. Respect explicit no-code, no-image, or no-web instructions.

## Validation
Run the representative, boundary, and integrity cases in `evals/quality-cases.json`; record observed evidence with PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED, or NOT TESTED. Structural checks alone do not establish factual exhaustiveness.
