# Quality profile — consultation

## Purpose
Acceptance criteria for `/consultation`.

## Checks
- Use supplied business evidence and explicitly labeled financial assumptions.
- Preserve source dates, units, geography, and budget constraints.
- Distinguish modeled return from observed results and flag legal-risk boundaries.
- Deliver advanced and requested grade-adjusted reporting without invented facts.

## Verification
Representative, boundary, and integrity cases require observed evidence. Mark each result PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED, or NOT TESTED. Structural validation alone is not proof of runtime behavior.
