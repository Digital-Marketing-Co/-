# Disability Engine compatibility quality profile

## Contract
The `disability` skill is a compatibility alias for `engines/disability/ENGINE.md`; P and D must invoke the same accessible workflow. Preserve the user's authorized scope, minimize interaction, and resume only unfinished work that is actually identifiable. The canonical engine governs if compatibility wording diverges.

## Evidence and safety
Verify accessible output on an iPhone-sized viewport where relevant, with keyboard/screen-reader equivalents for controls. Prefer direct safe action over unnecessary questions, but preserve external approval, security, privacy, and consent requirements. Never infer identity, disability certification, privileged access, or authorization to reveal secrets. Never invent prior actions, tests, connected integrations, or background execution.

## Acceptance
- P and D resolve to the same canonical engine behavior.
- An authorized task continues with minimal interaction and clear reporting of observed changes.
- Ambiguous, unavailable, high-risk, or approval-gated actions stop safely and report the exact blocker.
- Outputs are readable, accessible, and concise, with no unsupported claims of completion.

## Validation
Exercise all cases in `evals/quality-cases.json` against real observed output. Record PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED, or NOT TESTED, including artifacts and limitations. Structural QA alone does not prove real-device accessibility or integration behavior.
