# Spatial source research

Start with the supplied root place, corridor, institution set, or spatial topic. Record geography, period, network kind, exclusions, and research questions in scope.md. Nodes carry stable IDs, supplied names and variants, source dates, evidence, coordinates when supported, and uncertainty. Connections state the documented relation and use existing node IDs.

Prefer primary map sheets, survey records, official gazetteers, institutional collections, and scholarly cartography. Record source URLs, titles, dates, exact cited pages, keeper decisions, and geographic coverage in sources.jsonl. Keep node observations in nodes.jsonl and round results in rounds/round-N.md. Distinguish measured coordinates from conceptual relations and present disputed names or boundaries with attribution.

Default limits from workflow.md are depth four from the root, eighty keeper sources, and forty gazetteer records unless the user extends them. Close a branch after two successive targeted rounds produce no usable evidence. Keep connected nodes needed to locate the root within scope. Preserve gaps rather than inventing missing locations, citations, or survey values.

These are research instructions. The bundled adapter consumes completed records and the PDF wrapper renders them; neither searches sources nor conducts this research automatically.
