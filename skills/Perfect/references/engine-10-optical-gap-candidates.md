## Semantic deduplication decisions
- Angular, wavelength, and polarization multiplexing are distinct addressing dimensions, related under multiplexed holography but not interchangeable.
- Mutual coherence function mapping is distinct from coherence length visualization, though related.
- Coherence gating is a measurement/selection technique, not merely spectral color effects.
- Phase stability monitoring is diagnostic, not equivalent to phase retrieval.
- Shack–Hartmann sensing is a particular wavefront measurement technique, not wavefront correction itself.
- Digital holographic microscopy is an imaging application, not a general projection effect.
- Phase-shifting interferometry is a measurement technique, distinct from decorative interference fringes.
- Twin-image suppression is reconstruction artifact mitigation, distinct from zero-order diffraction suppression.
- Autofocus hologram reconstruction is a reconstruction variant, not an independent optical phenomenon.
- Speckle-averaged reconstruction is mitigation/reconstruction, related to Speckle Contrast Reduction but not a standalone visual effect.
