## Semantic audit notes
- Color-Crosstalk Compensation is a color calibration/control variant; distinct from Color Temperature Shifting.
- Wavelength-Dependent Magnification Correction is a geometric calibration variant; not synonymous with Chromatic Aberration.
- Chromatic Speckle Equalization is a color-specific mitigation variant of Speckle Contrast Reduction.
- Diffraction Efficiency Budgeting is an engineering metric/optimization, not Bragg Diffraction.
- Higher-Order Diffraction Filtering is order-selective suppression, related to Zero-Order Diffraction Suppression but different targets.
- Coherent Stray-Light Suppression is mitigation, related to Stray-Light Ghost Analysis (diagnosis).
- Phase Flicker Suppression is a temporal control technique, not Temporal Anti-Aliasing.
- Holographic Motion Blur Compensation is display-specific correction, not generic Motion-Compensated Interpolation.
- Temporal Speckle Decorrelation is a specific approach within Speckle Contrast Reduction.
- Laser Exposure Safety Budget is a hardware safety assessment, not a decorative visual effect.
- Pupil-Plane Irradiance Mapping is a measurement and safety visualization, not Eye-Box Expansion.
- Thermal Drift Compensation is calibration control, related to Hologram Calibration Drift Detection (diagnosis).
