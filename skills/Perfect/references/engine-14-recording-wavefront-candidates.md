## Cross-catalog semantic treatment
- Digital Holographic Data Storage is an information-storage application, not a browser visual effect.
- Shift multiplexing and peristrophic multiplexing are different addressing methods; retain as distinct subtypes of hologram multiplexing.
- Digital Optical Phase Conjugation is an adaptive optical method; it relates to Wavefront Aberration Correction but is not equivalent.
- Transmission Matrix Wavefront Shaping is a measured scattering-medium control technique, not generic Wavefront Propagation.
- Scattering-Medium Holographic Focusing is a target application of wavefront shaping; link as an application variant, not necessarily a unique underlying algorithm.
- Time-Resolved Digital Holography and Pump–Probe Holographic Imaging overlap in temporal imaging but differ in experimental acquisition and excitation.
- Single-Shot Off-Axis Phase Recovery is an algorithmic acquisition variant of Off-Axis Holographic Reconstruction; preserve alias and variant metadata.
