## Preliminary semantic decisions
- Acousto-Optic Holographic Modulation is a physical subtype of spatial light modulation, not an exact synonym.
- Photorefractive Hologram Recording differs from Volume Holographic Optical Elements (recording mechanism vs optical component).
- Electro-Holographic Refresh Control is hardware control, not a visual effect.
- Complex-Amplitude Hologram Encoding and Double-Phase Hologram Encoding are related but distinct algorithms.
- Off-Axis Holographic Reconstruction is a subtype of hologram reconstruction.
- Zero-Order Diffraction Suppression is mitigation, not Bragg Diffraction.
- Retinal Image Quality Estimation is a metric; Visual Acuity-Aware Rendering is adaptive rendering.
- Accommodation Cue Synthesis relates to Focal Plane Shift but adds synthesis of focus cues.
- Disocclusion Hole Filling is a variant of Depth-Guided Image Synthesis.
- Six-Degree-of-Freedom Anchor Drift Correction differs from World-Locked Content Reconciliation.
- Multi-User Hologram Registration differs from single-user Perspective Registration.
- Pose Prediction Uncertainty Visualization is diagnostic, not pose prediction.
