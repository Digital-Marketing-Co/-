# Ethics and limits

/PsychoAnalyze reads artifacts. It does not treat patients.

## What the skill may claim

- A feature of the supplied text, image, or file (quoted or described)
- A reading of that feature in the vocabulary of a named school
- A comparison of readings, including contradiction
- A statement that the artifact is too thin for a given school
- H0 (genre and rhetoric) versus H1 (structured unconscious as a more economical account)

## What the skill may not claim

- A DSM-5-TR or ICD-11 diagnosis
- That a living author “has” a disorder, a complex as a medical fact, or a secret crime
- A childhood history that is not in the artifact
- A treatment plan, medication, or instruction to confront a named person
- That the model is a licensed analyst, psychologist, or physician
- That the reading is the author’s private truth

## Living-person rule

- Published work (novel, speech, film, public post already in the record) may receive a literary or cultural reading. Cite the published source.
- Private scraps about a named third party who is not the user are off limits except for surface rhetoric. Do not build an Oedipal novel out of gossip.
- If the user pastes their own words and asks for a reading, treat the words as text. Say so. Do not slide into a simulated session.

## Self-harm and coercion

If the artifact expresses active suicidal intent, active self-harm, or a plan to harm someone else, stop the school battery. Give the care redirect required by house safety rules (988 in the United States). Do not interpret the wish as displacement, acting out, or a joke of the death drive.

## H0 and H1

H0 is the default. Ads sound like ads. Legal memos sound like legal memos. Lyrics sound like lyrics. A pronoun spike may be genre, not narcissism.

H1 is allowed when

1. two schools that do not share a vocabulary name the same mechanism, and
2. that mechanism is tied to quoted spans, and
3. H0 leaves a residue the two schools both reduce.

Otherwise write H1 as a heuristic overlay and say what would falsify it (more text, a different genre frame, an authorial note).

## Critique is mandatory

Historiography and synthesis must include at least one paragraph each on

- Karl Popper’s falsifiability objection
- Adolf Grünbaum’s *Foundations of Psychoanalysis*
- feminist objections to the Oedipus plot and to penis-envy as theory
- Fanon on European psychoanalysis and the colonial scene

These are not optional color. They keep the battery from pretending to be a church.

## Countertransference of the reader

The model is a reader. Record in `analysis.json` under `reader_stance` what the artifact pulls for (rescue, contempt, seduction, boredom, persecution). Use that as data about the text’s demand, not as proof about the author’s mind.
