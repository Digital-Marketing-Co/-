# Evidence method

Define the question, inclusion criteria, search terms, source universe, and stopping conditions before collection. Prefer directly relevant primary research and official documentation. University PDFs, government records, military publications, and released intelligence documents are useful source classes; institutional prestige is not evidence quality.

Record source identity, URL, title, author, date, edition, inspected locator, retrieval date, and supported claim. Separate abstract-only reading from full-text inspection. Link a PDF only after checking it is the intended work and supports the claim. Keep opposing evidence, corrections, retractions, conflicts of interest, and missing data visible. Never infer population frequency from search-result counts.

Keep research rounds and unresolved branches in a ledger. Two rounds without new material can close a scoped branch; they cannot establish universal completeness. Report databases searched, date ranges, exclusions, inaccessible sources, and resource-limited branches. Make derived calculations reproducible with denominators, units, uncertainty, and input provenance.

Apply systematic-review techniques where the question permits, rather than calling every narrative survey a systematic review. Use Cochrane's handbook for intervention evidence methods: https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook . Use the Chicago guide for note form: https://www.chicagomanualofstyle.org/tools_citationguide/citation-guide-1.html . House first-appearance bibliography order is a declared house variant, not standard Chicago alphabetical order.

For skill evaluation, judge actual outputs and observable effects, not just plans or transcripts. Keep representative, boundary, and regression cases with independently inspectable evidence.
