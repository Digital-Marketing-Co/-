# Pedagogy, usability, accessibility

The Folio page is built so a reader can stay with an argument, jump to a note, and return without hunting.

## Measure and type

- Letter page, inner margin 0.95 in, outer 0.90 in.
- Body 10 / 14.2 Literata on a measure of about 6.55 in — roughly 68–74 characters.
- First paragraph of a section has no indent. Later paragraphs indent 12 pt.
- Cited notes reprint in a page footnote band above the living copyright line.
- Justification is on. Do not force hyphenation by inserting soft hyphens by hand.

## Contrast and color

- Ink `#1A1916` on cream `#FBF7F0`.
- Muted chrome `#4A4742`.
- Links `#1F3A5F`, underlined only in running text URLs, not on superscripts.
- Body contrast exceeds WCAG 2.2 AAA against the cream.

## Structure a reader can use

- PDF outline (bookmarks) for every section.
- Named destinations `note-N` for every note.
- Superscript runs link to those destinations.
- Title-page house name is a URI link.
- Running header carries a shortened title so orientation survives a mid-argument page turn.
- Page numbers start after the title leaf.

## What not to do

- Do not set body copy in EB Garamond or Libre Franklin.
- Do not use a script, blackletter, or display italic for more than a title or kicker.
- Do not put figures over type.
- Do not emit emoji or replacement boxes.
- Do not number the bibliography. Order it by first appearance of each work.
