# Logo placement and copyright integration — quality profile

## Provenance and integrity
Use only a user-supplied logo or a logo asset whose path and contents have been verified in the canonical repository. Never infer a URL, invent an asset, or claim an external deployment. Preserve the logo's aspect ratio and clear space; do not crop the Earth mark or wordmark without authorization.

## Document integration
When invoked with /copyright, /4629, /banner, or /images, place the verified brand asset immediately below the single canonical copyright notice in a footer or copyright section. Maintain legible text, sufficient contrast, and a print-safe alternative. Respect each output medium's layout constraints and avoid duplicate marks and notices.

## QA protocol
Check that the logo is present at most once in each intended copyright section; that all legally required third-party notices remain; and that the logo is not distorted, occluded, or overlapping text. Test mobile, desktop, and print views where applicable. Record whether checks were actually executed; do not claim visual QA from structural validation alone.

## Missing asset handling
If no verified image asset is available, preserve the surrounding document and report the missing asset. Do not silently substitute an AI-generated approximation for the official mark.
