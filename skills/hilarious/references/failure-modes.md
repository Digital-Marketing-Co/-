# Failure modes (monograph Ch. 10)

Rewrite the story if any gate trips. Do not send a draft that fails closed.

## 10.1 No violation

Nothing surprising, discrepant, ambiguous, or norm-challenging. Familiarity flattened the premise. Add a real mismatch.

## 10.2 Violation without benignness

Threat, cruelty, disgust, sacred-value conflict, or real injury dominates. Slapstick becomes horror when survivability cues vanish. Soften stakes, fictionalize, self-target, or change the target to a system.

## 10.3 Benignness without violation

Safe and unsurprising is pleasant, not funny. Add representational friction.

## 10.4 Insufficient shared knowledge

Puns, parody, scientific jokes, political satire, memes, and callbacks need a recoverable script. If the reader cannot have that script from the story itself plus ordinary adult knowledge, plant the script in the setup.

## 10.5 Excessive processing cost

The inferential bridge is too obscure. If a paragraph of explanation would be required, the punch failed. Compress or plant a clearer trigger.

## 10.6 Power and social threat

Humor from a high-status speaker at a low-status target stops feeling optional. Do not write downward humiliation of a subordinate, patient, student, or named private person. Self-directed status drops are allowed.

## Extra gates for this skill

- Explaining the joke inside the story
- A list of funny nouns instead of a scene
- A lecture from the monograph instead of a story
- A punch that only works if the reader already hates a named group
