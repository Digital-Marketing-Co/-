# Scope

Working title: Stigma Ranked: United States Statistics and a Global Country Atlas Ordered by GDP per Capita

Research questions:
1. How should stigma be defined for cross-national statistics?
2. What is the US statistical distribution of major stigmas?
3. Which domains rank globally on published instruments?
4. How do cultural histories produce national stigma repertoires?
5. Does GDP per capita systematically reduce public stigma?
6. Where do datasets fail to saturate?

Inclusion: published multi-country instruments after 2000; World Bank/IMF GDP; no invented ethnography.
Exclusion: a single composite "stigma index of nations."
