# Round 1 — field map

Established: pulsed microwave energy can be heard (Frey 1962). Consensus mechanism after Foster and Finch 1974 and Guy et al. 1975 is thermoelastic expansion and bone conduction to the cochlea, not direct EM resonance of CN VIII.
Continuous-wave does not produce the classic microwave hearing effect.
Not established: fluent voices from a penetrating analogue intermodulated carrier via hydroxyapatite resonance of the auditory or optic nerve.
