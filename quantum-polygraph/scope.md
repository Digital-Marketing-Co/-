# Scope — Quantum Polygraph

Working title: The Quantum Polygraph: Bell Tests as Certificates against Classical Mimicry

Research questions
1. What does the press phrase "quantum lie detector" name in 2024–2026 physics, and what must it not name?
2. How does Bell's 1964 theorem, via CHSH and many-body energy witnesses, certify that a device is not a classical fake?
3. What did Wang et al., Phys. Rev. X 15, 021024 (2025) actually measure on 73 and 24 superconducting qubits?
4. How does that certificate differ from the NRC 2003 assessment of physiological polygraphy?
5. Which neighboring instruments (cryptographic proofs of quantumness, quantum position verification, Hardy pedagogy, device-independent protocols) belong in the same family?
6. What remains open: loopholes at scale, human-deception claims, and the misuse of the metaphor.
