# Scope

Working title: Beauty Ranked: Landscape, Heritage, and Resort Saturation in a World Atlas Ordered by GDP per Capita

Research questions:
1. How should “beauty” be operationalized for a cross-national atlas without inventing a composite scenic index no agency maintains?
2. What does IMF April 2026 nominal GDP per capita actually rank, and where do Ireland, Luxembourg, and city-states distort living-standard comparisons?
3. How do UN Tourism arrivals, STR occupancy, and UNESCO World Heritage counts jointly describe resort and heritage saturation?
4. Which high-income states convert landscape into a high-yield, low-volume product, and which convert it into mass visitation?
5. Where do middle- and low-income states hold first-order beauty assets with thin formal resort plant?
6. What remains unmeasured after official statistics: carrying capacity, resident consent, and aesthetic disagreement?

Inclusion: UN members and separately published IMF/World Bank economies; official tourism and heritage series after 2000; generated plates labeled as illustrations.
Exclusion: a single numeric “beauty score of nations”; photoreal portraits of private persons; invented visitor counts.
