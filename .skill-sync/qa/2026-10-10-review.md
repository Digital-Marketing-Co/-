The 177 per-skill scenario definitions are future regression criteria and remain explicitly NOT TESTED. Live image generation, external provider calls, device-specific ringtone import, interactive widgets, full website crawling, authenticated Bitly writes, live deployment, and paid model calls were not run. No numerical coverage percentage is claimed. No paid inference was used for validation. This is a substantial reviewed improvement with explicit remaining test surfaces; it is not a claim of universal completeness.
